ABOUT & CREDITS
An open-world pixel action game. Explore Bone Shore, fight the Mushrooms of the forest, the Insect Priests of the swamp and the Pirates of the beach, hunt the rare Hippo and Crocodile bosses, complete the unlock quests and level up all five Elemental Ninjas.

Character and enemy models: original pixel art by the game's author. Everything else — map, effects, UI and sound — is drawn and synthesized in code inside this single file. No external assets.
